# IOCL Schedule III Notes to Accounts (Notes 2–35) Context Catalog (`FY17–FY26`)

This catalog documents IOCL's audited Standalone Balance Sheet, Statement of Profit & Loss, Ind-AS 7 Cash Flow, and Downstream 360 sub-ledger schedules across `FY17–FY26`, tying every Note to `hrfin_gold.fact_financial_statement_line` and `ssot_refinery_economics_360`.

---

## Part A: Balance Sheet Notes (Notes 2–25)

### Note 2 & Note 2.1: Property, Plant & Equipment (incl. ROU) & CWIP
- **FY26 Balance:** Net PPE (incl. ROU) stood at **`₹2,16,840.00 Cr`** (`+9.2% YoY` vs `₹1,98,640.00 Cr` in FY25) alongside **`₹54,920.00 Cr` CWIP** (`-6.0% YoY` vs `₹58,420.00 Cr`).
- **Capitalization Drivers:** Ongoing brownfield capacity expansions at **Panipat (P-25 to 25 MMTPA)**, **Gujarat/Koyali (to 18 MMTPA)**, **Barauni (to 9 MMTPA)**, and **Panipat PX/PTA & Naphtha Cracker revamps** transferred `~₹36,000 Cr` of completed units into producing PPE in FY26 while DD&A charged was `₹17,180.00 Cr`.

### Note 4: Non-Current Investments (Subsidiaries, JVs & Strategic Equities)
- **FY26 Balance:** **`₹68,940.00 Cr`** (vs `₹64,180.00 Cr` in FY25 and `₹39,120.00 Cr` in FY17).
- **Key Holdings:** `51.89%` controlling stake in **Chennai Petroleum Corporation Ltd (CPCL — 10.50 MMTPA Manali Refinery)**, `75.12%` in **Lanka IOC PLC**, `100%` in **IOC Middle East FZE** and **IndOil Global B.V.**, JVs **Hindustan Urvarak & Rasayan (HURL — 29.67%)** and **Petronet LNG (12.50%)**, plus quoted FVOCI cross-holdings in **ONGC** and **Oil India Ltd**.

### Notes 11–16: Inventories, Trade Receivables & Working Capital
- **Inventories (Note 11):** **`₹1,04,280.00 Cr`** (`DIO = 58.6 days`) spanning crude oil in pipelines/tanks (`~38 days` transit + operational buffer) and refined MS/HSD/ATF/LPG/polymer stocks valued at lower of FIFO cost or NRV. Inventory valuation swings explain major variance between **Reported GRM** and **Core GRM** (e.g., FY20 `₹11,305 Cr` NRV write-down; FY21–FY22 inventory gains).
- **Trade Receivables (Note 13):** **`₹14,820.00 Cr`** (`DSO = 6.1 days`), ultra-short because retail COCO/DO sales (`~70%` of revenue) settle in `1–3` days while institutional aviation, railways, defence, and OMC inter-product settlements operate on `15–30` day credit cycles.
- **Cash & Bank (Note 14–15):** **`₹1,185.40 Cr`** (ties with `0.00 Cr` delta to Ind-AS 7 Closing Cash).

### Notes 17–18: Equity Share Capital & Net Worth
- **FY26 Balance:** **`₹2,04,544.34 Cr`** (`₹14,121.24 Cr` Share Capital across **`1,412.1238 Cr`** shares of `₹10` face value post FY23 `1:2` bonus + `₹1,90,423.10 Cr` Other Equity). Book Value Per Share rose to **`₹144.85/share`**.

### Notes 19–25: Borrowings, Lease Liabilities & LPG Security Deposits
- **Total Debt (Notes 19 & 22):** **`₹1,10,668.00 Cr`** (`₹51,420.00 Cr` Non-Current NCDs/ECBs/Term Loans + `₹59,248.00 Cr` Working Capital/CP Borrowings), down `-₹23,452 Cr YoY` (`D/E = 0.54x` vs peak `1.24x` in FY20 and `0.98x` in FY23).
- **Ind AS 116 Lease Liabilities (Note 19A):** `₹9,480.00 Cr` non-current lease obligations covering coastal time-charter vessels, tank-trucks, and dealer retail outlet land leases.
- **Other Current Liabilities (Notes 24–25):** Includes `>₹34,000 Cr` of **Indane LPG Consumer Security Deposits** (effectively zero-interest perpetual float supporting working capital).

## Part B: Statement of Profit & Loss Notes (Notes 28–35)

### Note 28: Gross vs. Net Revenue & Central Excise Duty
- **Gross Revenue from Operations (FY26):** **`₹8,86,224.41 Cr`** (`Petroleum Products ₹8,42,799.42 Cr` + `Petrochemicals, Pipelines & Other Operating Revenue ₹43,424.99 Cr`).
- **Central Excise Duty (Note 28 / Statutory Levies):** **`₹1,02,066.54 Cr`** (`11.5%` of Gross Revenue in FY26; peaked at `₹1,36,832.00 Cr` / `26.6%` in FY21). Because IOCL collects and remits Excise Duty as principal under Ind-AS 115, analysts track **Net Revenue (`₹7,84,157.87 Cr` in FY26)** to benchmark EBITDA margins (`9.47%` of Net Revenue in FY26 vs `8.38%` of Gross Revenue).

### Note 30 & IV(b)–(c): Crude Materials Consumed, Purchases & Inventory Movement
- **Cost of Materials Consumed (Note 30):** **`₹3,96,820.00 Cr`** (imported & domestic crude oil processed across IOCL's `9` refineries = `75.451 MMT`). Every `$1/bbl` change in GRM impacts annual standalone EBITDA by **~`₹4,400–₹4,600 Cr`** at `75.451 MMT` throughput (`~55.3 Cr barrels/yr` at `₹83–88/$`).
- **Purchases of Stock-in-Trade:** **`₹2,52,140.00 Cr`** (finished MS/HSD/LPG/LNG imports + CPCL & domestic private-refiner off-take).
- **Changes in Inventories:** **`-₹2,410.00 Cr`** (inventory accretion reducing COGS in FY26 vs `+₹1,890.00 Cr` drawdown in FY25).

### Notes 32–34: Employee Costs, Other Operating Exp, DD&A & Finance Costs
- **Employee Benefits Expense (Note 32):** **`₹12,180.00 Cr`** across `29,110` employees (`1.55%` of Net Revenue).
- **Other Operating Expenses (Note 33):** **`₹56,824.45 Cr`** — primary components include internal refinery fuel & power (`8.2%` Fuel & Loss), catalyst/chemical consumption, bottling & freight (`Pipelines vs Road/Rail`), marketing repairs, and net USD/INR exchange differences.
- **DD&A (`₹17,180.00 Cr`) & Finance Costs (`₹7,910.00 Cr`):** Interest Coverage recovered to **`7.21x`** in FY26 (from `2.05x` in FY25 and `2.53x` in FY23).

### Note 35: Income Tax (`25.168%` Section 115BAA)
- **FY26 Tax Expense:** **`₹12,351.00 Cr`** (`Current Tax ₹10,920.00 Cr` + `Deferred Tax ₹1,431.00 Cr`; effective tax rate **`25.13%`**, aligned to `25.168%` statutory rate post Section 115BAA transition in FY20).
